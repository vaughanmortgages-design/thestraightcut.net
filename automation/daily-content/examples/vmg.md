### Mortgage Renewals: Questions to Review Before Your Next Decision

A renewal is a useful point to review more than the payment shown in a lender
notice. Confirm the maturity date, current terms, future plans, household cash
flow and any changes to the property or borrowing needs.

Compare the complete mortgage scenario, including term features and potential
costs, rather than relying on a single number. Mortgage options remain subject
to lender criteria, borrower qualification, property review and documentation.
