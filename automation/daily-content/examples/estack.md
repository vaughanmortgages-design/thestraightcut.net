### Comparing Bullion Offers Without Chasing the Headline

Start with the exact metal, form, weight and purity shown by the dealer. Then
review the current price, availability, shipping, payment terms and storage
considerations directly on the approved merchant page. No price or availability
should be assumed from an older post.
