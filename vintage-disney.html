Before you buy

Collect first. Regret never.

Vintage marketplaces reward careful buyers. These checks matter more than a flashy listing title.

01

Compare sold listings

Asking price is not market value. Check completed sales for similar condition and year.

02

Inspect every photo

Look for maker marks, dates, damage, repairs, fading and whether the seller uses actual photos.

03

Check the seller

Review feedback, selling history, returns and how clearly the condition is described.

04

Demand provenance

For animation art, props and expensive pieces, documentation matters. A certificate alone is not proof.

05

Count the full cost

Add shipping, tax, duties and restoration before deciding what the piece is worth to you.

06

Walk away from pressure

Rare does not mean buy now. If photos or answers are weak, wait for the next listing.
